# 07 Quality Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist helps Ship Design review generated artifacts before they move into Figma, prototyping, landing page creation, or development.

## Project Brief Quality

- The product idea is clear.
- The target user is named.
- The user problem is specific.
- The product promise is understandable.
- The product category is identified.
- Key constraints are listed.
- Open questions are captured.

## Product Strategy Quality

- The audience is specific enough to design for.
- The problem is not too broad.
- The value proposition is clear.
- MVP scope is realistic.
- Success metrics are measurable.
- Differentiation is explained.

## UX Research Quality

- Assumptions are listed.
- Research questions are practical.
- Target participants are identified.
- Risks are stated plainly.
- The research plan fits the MVP stage.

## User Flow Quality

- Each flow starts with a user goal.
- Each flow has a clear end.
- Decision points are included.
- Error paths are considered.
- Empty states are considered.
- The flows can be turned into screens.

## Screen Inventory Quality

- Every screen has a purpose.
- Important states are listed.
- Navigation is understandable.
- Screen names are clear.
- No obvious screens are missing.
- The list is not bloated with unnecessary screens.

## Design System Quality

- Color roles are defined.
- Typography roles are defined.
- Core components are listed.
- States are included.
- Accessibility is considered.
- The style matches the dark technical visual direction.

## Figma Structure Quality

- Pages are clearly named.
- Frame names are practical.
- Component names are consistent.
- Prototype paths are planned.
- Handoff sections are included.
- A designer could continue from the structure.

## Landing Page Quality

- The audience understands the product quickly.
- The headline is specific.
- The page explains the transformation from idea to design package.
- Benefits are concrete.
- The call to action is clear.
- Screens or product visuals are planned.

## Developer Handoff Quality

- Features are described clearly.
- Screens and states are listed.
- Components are named.
- Data requirements are included.
- Edge cases are captured.
- Open questions are visible.

## Final Package Quality

- The artifacts agree with each other.
- The package tells one coherent product story.
- The output is useful without extra explanation.
- The next step is obvious.
- The user can move into Figma or implementation with confidence.
